How to set up your signature

1Fill in your details

Enter your name, job title, email and (optional) phone on the left. The preview updates as you type.

2Copy the signature

Click Copy signature. Don't select and copy the preview by hand, the button copies it with the right formatting.

3Paste it into your email app

Follow the steps for your app below, paste, and save. Send yourself a test email to check it.

Outlook desktop (Windows)

  1. File → Options → Mail → Signatures…
  2. Click New, name it "SGFX" and click OK.
  3. Click in the edit box and press Ctrl + V.
  4. On the right, set New messages and Replies/forwards to "SGFX", then click OK.

New Outlook / Outlook on the web

  1. ⚙️ Settings → Accounts → Signatures → + New signature.
  2. Name it "SGFX" and paste into the box.
  3. Set it as the default for new messages and replies/forwards, then click Save.

Outlook for Mac

  1. Outlook → Settings → Signatures → +.
  2. Name it "SGFX", paste into the box and close the window to save.
  3. Under Choose default signature, pick "SGFX" for new messages and replies.

Gmail

  1. ⚙️ → See all settings → General → Signature → Create new.
  2. Name it "SGFX" and paste into the box.
  3. Under Signature defaults, pick "SGFX" for new emails and replies, then click Save Changes at the bottom.

Apple Mail

  1. Open this page in Safari and click Copy signature.
  2. Mail → Settings → Signatures → +.
  3. Untick "Always match my default message font", then paste into the right-hand panel.

Gmail / Outlook on your phone

  1. Phone apps only support plain text signatures.
  2. Set up the signature on your computer first. Emails you send from the web or desktop app will use it.

Good to know

  • Replacing an old signature? Delete the old WiseStamp signature so you don't end up with two, or paste over it.
  • Logo or icons not showing? Click "Download pictures" / "Show images" at the top of the email. They load from the internet, so they won't show while you're offline.
  • Details changed? Come back to this page, update your details, copy again and paste over your old signature.
  • Don't edit the signature inside Outlook or Gmail. Small edits there can break the layout. Make changes here and copy again.
